Privacy Policy: Yaali's Island Quest

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Yaali's Island Quest (package com.lakshmaji.yaali) is a game made by Lakshmaji. This page explains what the game does with your information. In short: it collects nothing.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for an account, your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. It does not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and it does not request internet or any other Android permission.

Game progress

Your progress (levels finished, coins, upgrades and settings) is saved only on your device, in the game's own private storage. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Children

The game is suitable for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Google Play

Buying and downloading the game is handled by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your payment details.

Changes

If a future version adds features that use your information (for example ads or online saves), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
