Yaali's Island Quest

A cozy 3D island adventure. Guide Yaali across 21 lands, from flowery meadow isles to glaciers, deserts and torch-lit caves, find the keys, outsmart the monsters and reach the gate.

Get it on Google Play

The adventure

  • 21 levels: meadow and autumn isles, desert dunes, a glacier coast, the tundra, a taiga forest, sunken sands, snowy peaks and Torchlight Caverns
  • Folk-tale lands of China, Mongolia, Vietnam and Cambodia, each with its own puzzle, two villains from local tales, a folk tool and three treasures
  • Follow the signposts to the keys, fight demons and bosses, and eat veggies to heal
  • Collect gold from chests and upgrade your powers at the Forge
  • Climb, hide, swim and dive with Yaali
  • Escape an unbeatable T-rex in the dino egg chase

Made for every phone

  • Low, Medium and High graphics settings
  • Touch controls and four camera views, from a bird's-eye look at the whole island to Yaali's own eyes
  • Plays fully offline; progress saves on your phone
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collected
